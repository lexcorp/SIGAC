# VIS-005 — Radius & Elevation

Radius:
- sm 6
- md 10
- lg 16
- brand-xl 24+

Operational surfaces prefer md.
Large organic radii reserved for brand experience.

Elevation is subtle; hierarchy should rely more on surface and spacing than shadows.
