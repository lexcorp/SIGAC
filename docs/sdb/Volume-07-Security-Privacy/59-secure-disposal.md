# SEC-059 — Secure Disposal

For temporary digital artifacts:
- staging files;
- exports;
- temp reports;
- old secrets;
- decommissioned disks;
- backups at end-of-life.

Use platform-appropriate secure disposal. Do not confuse deletion of SIGAC technical artifacts with archival disposition of the physical clinical expediente.
